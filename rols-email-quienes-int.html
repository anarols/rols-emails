Today
Today, four generations on, we're still here. Carmen, Fernando and Marta lead Rols, keeping a way of working built on in-house manufacturing, made-to-measure design and craftsmanship.

More than 100 years on, we're still weaving in Spain.

Sustainability

Designing better is also making better

Materials and manufacturing

At Rols we believe design, manufacturing and sustainability go hand in hand. That's why we work to reduce the use of virgin fibres, incorporate recycled materials and develop new solutions for our rugs.

B Corp certification

Our commitment has been recognised with B Corp certification, which distinguishes companies that meet high standards of social and environmental impact.

Circular economy

And we keep moving forward: we don't want a rug to end when its useful life is over. Our goal is to create rugs made from recycled materials and designed to be recycled again.

From waste to rug. From rug to a new cycle.
